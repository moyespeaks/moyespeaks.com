MENTORSHIP & GIVING

Some young people need more than a program.

Mentorship creates the time and relationship for deeper work: consistency, accountability, exposure, conversation and someone who keeps showing up.

WHY MENTORSHIP

Presence changes what is possible.

Programs can create momentum. Mentorship helps sustain it. The goal is to help young people build identity, make healthier choices, imagine a larger future and take practical steps toward it.

SUPPORT THE WORK

Help create more access.

Giving helps expand the time, experiences and opportunities available to young people who benefit from long-term support beyond a single workshop or school engagement.
