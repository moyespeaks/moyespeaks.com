ABOUT GODFREY

Years in the room. A lifetime of purpose.

Life On Purpose grew out of years spent listening to young people, challenging the stories that limit them, and helping them see that their next choice can change their direction.

FOUNDER · SPEAKER · YOUTH DEVELOPER

The person behind the program.

Godfrey Moye III has spent more than fifteen years in youth development, working with young people across New York City. His approach combines story, direct conversation, self-reflection and practical decision-making so students can understand who they are and move toward a future they can own.

He holds a B.A. in Youth Ministry Leadership from Eastern University. In 2013 he founded B4Men, a middle-school boys mentorship program, and later founded and launched GZ Life at the Living Redemption Youth Opportunity Hub in Harlem, where the program remains active today.
