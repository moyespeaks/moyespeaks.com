ABOUT GODFREY

A life built around reaching people.

Actor. Singer. Speaker. Mentor. Program builder. Different stages, classrooms and communities — one purpose: helping people see what is possible and choose what comes next.

FOUNDER · SPEAKER · ACTOR · YOUTH DEVELOPER

Godfrey has spent his life in rooms where words matter.

Godfrey Moye has worked with young people in juvenile halls, camps, group homes, public and private schools, and colleges — speaking to students from Los Angeles to New York City about choices, identity, direction, and what is possible for their lives.

His background extends far beyond youth development. As a professional actor, Godfrey has appeared in more than 30 commercials, bringing years of experience in storytelling, performance, and connecting with an audience. As a singer and performer, his work has taken him overseas. As a speaker, mentor, and program builder, he has spent years learning how to reach people from dramatically different backgrounds.

Those experiences eventually began pointing in the same direction: purpose.

Life On Purpose is not simply a program Godfrey created. It is the intersection of what he has spent his life doing: communicating, performing, mentoring, listening, and helping people recognize that where they are today does not have to determine where they go next.

Through Moye Speaks and Life On Purpose, Godfrey brings that experience into schools and organizations to help young people discover who they are, see what’s possible, and choose what’s next.

Godfrey holds a B.A. in Youth Ministry Leadership from Eastern University. In 2013 he founded B4Men, a middle-school boys mentorship program, and later founded and launched GZ Life at the Living Redemption Youth Opportunity Hub in Harlem.
